# Hospital bed availability in Lagos

Looking for a hospital with bed space in Lagos? OpenBed shows participating hospitals' own reports, including the ward category and when the count was last updated. [Open the live hospital list](https://openbed.ng/). Call the hospital before travelling or arranging a referral: a report is indicative, not a reservation.

## Find a hospital by area or near me

Choose a Lagos local government area to use its reference point as the starting point for distance comparisons. This does not restrict the list to hospitals inside that area and does not guarantee there is a participating hospital there. Near me uses your device location only in your browser.

Areas such as Ikeja, Lekki, Victoria Island, Ikoyi, Apapa, Surulere, Yaba, Ajah and mainland Lagos are common starting points for hospital searches. Use the actual area selector or Near me; neighbourhood names are not additional OpenBed filters. Distance is straight-line distance, not a route, journey time or ambulance arrival estimate.

## Choose the ward you need to check

Search emergency (A&E), adult ICU, children's ICU (PICU), adult medical, children's ward, surgical, operating theatre, maternity, newborn ICU (NICU) or special care baby unit (SCBU) reports. [See all ward categories and search links](https://openbed.ng/hospital-bed-availability-nigeria).

A maternity report does not confirm delivery services or staffing, and an ICU report does not confirm ventilators or specialist availability. Tell the receiving hospital what the clinical team has requested and let it confirm what it can provide.

## Check freshness before calling

Read the update time next to each report. No recent report, an old count and a report of not admitting are not interchangeable. A hospital missing from OpenBed may still have capacity; the list includes only participating facilities' reports.

The order can reflect report freshness or straight-line distance, depending on your chosen controls. It does not rank clinical suitability, hospital quality or likelihood of admission.

## Hospital admission and referrals in Lagos

Call the facility to confirm current capacity and its admission process. Referring and receiving clinicians coordinate any patient transfer directly. OpenBed does not book a bed, arrange transport, handle patient details or quote admission costs.

## Is this a list of all Lagos hospitals?

No. It shows hospitals currently participating and publishing reports. Public hospitals, private hospitals, teaching hospitals and general hospitals should not be assumed to be listed or reporting without checking the live list.

[How OpenBed works](https://openbed.ng/how-it-works) explains who updates reports. [Hospitals can learn about joining here](https://openbed.ng/hospital-bed-reporting).

In an emergency, call 112 or 767. Do not delay emergency care while checking reports. Call the facility before you travel.
