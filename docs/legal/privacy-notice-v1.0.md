# OpenBed privacy notice

*Version 1.0. Effective from the date it is first published at openbed.ng/privacy.*

## Who runs OpenBed

OpenBed is run by **Paddie Health Ltd**, a company registered in Nigeria (RC 9594167), of **1A Takoradi Road, Apapa, Lagos, Nigeria**. Paddie Health Ltd is the data controller for everything described here.

- Questions and requests about your data: **hello@openbed.ng**
- The Data Protection Officer: **hello@openbed.ng**, with "Data Protection Officer" in the subject line
- Ward support: support@openbed.ng
- Security reports: security@openbed.ng

## What OpenBed does not do

- It holds **no patient information** and asks for none.
- It does not ask for or record **your location**.
- It sets **no cookies**, stores nothing in your browser, and runs **no analytics or advertising trackers**.
- It does **not sell or share** personal data, and uses no one's details for marketing.
- It makes **no automated decisions** about anyone.

## Transfers outside Nigeria

All three providers operate outside Nigeria. Each transfer relies on the provider's data-processing agreement, including its standard contractual clauses, and on data-protection law in the destination that gives substantially similar protection (NDPA ss.41–42). The basis for each transfer is recorded as s.41(2) requires. A hospital's named contact also consents to the transfer when their details are given. You can ask for details at hello@openbed.ng.

## Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask to:

- see the personal data held about you, and get a copy (s.34);
- have it corrected, or deleted (s.34);
- have its use restricted (s.34);
- object to its use (s.36);
- receive it in a portable form (s.38).

Write to **hello@openbed.ng**. You will get an answer within **30 days**, and it costs nothing. OpenBed may need to confirm who you are first.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how your data is handled, write to hello@openbed.ng first; the Data Protection Officer will reply within 30 days. **You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission** at any time (NDPA s.46):

## How the data is protected

OpenBed has no passwords to steal: wards sign in with single-use email links. Every database table is locked so that each ward sees only its own facility, and only the platform operator can read a hospital's contact details. Operator access sits behind two-factor sign-in. If a breach puts your rights at risk, the Commission is told within 72 hours and you are told without delay (s.40).
